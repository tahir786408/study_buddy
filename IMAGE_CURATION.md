# Image Curation Notes

## Final image set (the keepers)
- **Home page:** Custom SVG icon — overlapping flashcards + a checkmark badge (purple/gray palette)
- **Generate page:** Custom SVG icon — notes document → arrow → quiz card, representing the AI transformation
- **Study page:** Custom SVG icon — stacked flashcards with progress dots
- **About page:** Real personal photo (profile.jpeg), circular crop

## What I rejected, and why

I initially considered searching for AI-generated or stock illustrations (e.g. "student studying with laptop" style images) for the Home and Generate pages. I rejected this direction for two reasons: first, stock images returned by an image search are copyrighted and not legally reusable; second, most AI-illustration styles default to a busy, colorful "corporate-illustration" look that would have clashed with the tight 4-color palette already set in the Identity Kit — matching it exactly would have taken many regeneration attempts, and the extra visual noise risked competing with the page's actual message instead of supporting it (the "frame, not upstage" principle).

## Where I chose a real capture over AI

For the About page, I used a real photo of myself rather than generating an avatar or illustration. The brief is a "proof of work" — a generated stand-in for a real person undermines the credibility the page is trying to build, since anyone AI-generating their own likeness would call the authenticity of the whole proof statement into question.

## Consistency check
All three custom icons (Home, Generate, Study) reuse the exact same two colors from the Identity Kit — `#7C3AED` (primary purple) and `#EDE9FE` (light purple) — plus white for internal details. No new colors were introduced for any generated visual, so the set reads as one style rather than three separate pieces.
